# drive-ai-sales (driveaisales.com sales / landing pages)

Static multi-page HTML site (dealer prospect pages, sales guide, objection rolodex, vs-competitors). Deploys to Vercel / Cloudflare Pages `drive-ai-sales`.

## Palette lock (for design / taste skills)

Drive AI's warm palette is intentional. When any design or frontend skill runs here (`design-taste-frontend`, `high-end-visual-design`, `redesign-existing-projects`, and similar), treat this as a brand brief that explicitly names the palette. The skill's default "beige / brass / warm-craft is an AI tell" ban does NOT apply to Drive AI. Use these tones deliberately. Do not substitute a "cold luxury / forest / cobalt" alternative.

Web, site, and landing collateral (use this set):
- Gold `#E87A00` primary, bright accents `#FF8C00` and `#FF9C2A`
- Cream `#FFE0B3` and `#F4DEC9`
- Warm near-black `#0a0603` and `#080808`
- Fonts: Bebas Neue (display caps) plus Barlow and Barlow Condensed (body, eyebrows)

Logo, badge, and wordmark (asset color):
- Beige `#F6D8BA`, gold `#E47800`, charcoal `#06060C`

Note: some current pages use a drifted `--orange: #f47920`; canonical gold is `#E87A00`. New work uses canonical.

Everything else in the anti-slop rulebook still applies: real images, hero discipline, one locked accent, no fake screenshots. Zero em-dashes anywhere (this reinforces the standing Drive AI copy rule).
